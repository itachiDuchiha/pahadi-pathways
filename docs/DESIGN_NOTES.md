# Design Notes

## Brand

- Brand Name: The Himalayan Compass
- Launch Focus: Himachal Pradesh
- Future Expansion: Other Himalayan destinations

---

## Design Style

- Clean and premium.
- Minimalist layout.
- Nature-inspired colors.
- Large hero images.
- Modern rounded components.
- Mobile-first responsive design.

---

## Homepage Goals

- Inspire visitors with photography.
- Make trip planning simple.
- Encourage enquiries instead of overwhelming users.
- Keep navigation minimal and intuitive.
